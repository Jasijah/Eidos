- [ ] Start on the product story: Turn Your Camera Off. Stay Present. Your Voice Stays Yours.
- [ ] Show Avatar Studio consent and local identity generation.

- [ ] Show system diagnostics and privacy deletion/export controls.

- [ ] Open Founder Dashboard and explain the closed beta success thresholds.
- [ ] State current limitations plainly: unsigned beta build, OBS route, placeholder photoreal generation, no voice cloning.
- [ ] End with closed beta learning goals and the native camera/photoreal roadmap.
