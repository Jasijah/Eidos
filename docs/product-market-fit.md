# Product-Market Fit

After five completed sessions, ask the Sean Ellis question: “If Eidos disappeared tomorrow, how disappointed would you be?” The primary signal is the percentage answering **Very disappointed**. A directional PMF threshold is 40%, but only among users who completed real sessions.

Willingness-to-pay choices are $5, $10, $20 per month, enterprise only, or no. Track the distribution by cohort and pair it with actual repeated camera-off use. Stated willingness without repeated use is not product-market fit.

Interview very-disappointed users to identify the essential use case. Interview churned or low-trust users to understand setup, realism, disclosure, or privacy objections. Do not optimize avatar novelty at the expense of presence reliability.
