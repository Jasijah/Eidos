# Eidos Product Vision

Eidos is a realistic AI presence layer for people who want to participate in live video calls without always appearing on camera. The product keeps the user's real voice and replaces only the visual feed with a professional human visual stand-in.

## Core Principle

Eidos is not voice cloning, not a synthetic speaker, and not a cartoon avatar product. The user speaks normally, and Eidos provides a realistic visual stand-in that lip-syncs and expresses presence.

## User Experience

1. A user creates a profile and chooses or creates an avatar.
2. The user optionally trains gesture memory with the camera enabled.
3. During calls, the avatar lip-syncs to live microphone volume.
4. If the camera is enabled, the avatar mirrors basic head position, nodding, blinking, smiling, and tilt.
5. If the camera is off, Voice-Only Presence Mode animates the avatar from saved gesture patterns.
6. The user can choose Professional, Casual, Creator, or Low Energy / Sick Day behavior.
7. Privacy controls stay visible, including Avatar Mode Active disclosure and memory deletion.

## Target Users

- Remote workers who need camera flexibility without disappearing from a meeting.
- Creators and presenters who want a recognizable visual presence.
- Users who are sick, fatigued, traveling, or in a private environment.
- Teams with accessibility and privacy needs around video presence.

## Product Boundaries

- The first product should be local-first where possible.
- Camera use should be opt-in and easy to disable.
- Gesture memory should be understandable and deletable.
- The avatar should clearly represent a visual mode, not deceive participants into thinking it is raw camera footage.
- The avatar should feel like a professional video-call presence layer rather than a toy, emoji, or game character.

## Long-Term Direction

Eidos should become a trustworthy presence layer for video communication: realistic enough to communicate attention and affect, transparent enough to preserve trust, and private enough for everyday work.
