# Known Issues

- OBS Virtual Camera is required; Eidos does not yet install a native camera device.
- Desktop installers are unsigned development artifacts and may trigger operating-system warnings.
- Safari and Firefox may use reduced MediaPipe or media-device behavior compared with Chrome and Edge.
- The local realistic portrait provider is not a full neural or production photoreal avatar generator.
- External photoreal, Ready Player Me, and some VRM provider paths remain adapters or controlled stubs.
- System Check reports OBS as guided/manual because reliable cross-platform OBS process detection is not yet bundled.
- Auto-update checks are disabled in development and require a signed packaged release feed.
- Help Center video and Discord community links are placeholders.
- Founder metrics are local to one installation until anonymized exports are aggregated.
