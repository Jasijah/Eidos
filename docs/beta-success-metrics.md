# Beta Success Metrics

- **Closed beta user:** accepted invite or locally completed beta enrollment.
- **Weekly active user:** completed at least one presence session in the last seven days.
- **Onboarding completion:** account/presence introduction, avatar creation, and training completion events all exist.
- **Avatar creation rate:** users who create an avatar divided by users who complete account onboarding.
- **Voice-only adoption:** completed sessions with camera off divided by all completed sessions.
- **Average presence quality:** mean developer evaluator score across completed sessions.
- **Naturalness:** mean model naturalness plus separate user-rated naturalness. Never merge them without labeling.
- **Trust:** percent of feedback responses marking the experience trusted.
- **Crash-free sessions:** completed sessions without a captured crash divided by sessions started.
- **Activation:** onboarding completed and first presence session completed within 48 hours.

Recommended targets: onboarding >=70%, first-session activation >=60%, camera-off adoption >=60%, median session >=10 minutes, trust >=80%, user naturalness >=4/5, crash-free sessions >=98%.
