# Eidos Beta Readiness Report

Date: June 25, 2026

## Launch scope

Eidos is ready for a controlled 10-20 person closed beta where participants receive direct onboarding and use OBS Virtual Camera. The browser and desktop products support onboarding, local avatar identity creation, 60-second presence training, camera-off voice-only behavior, practice sessions, diagnostics, feedback, referrals, and local founder metrics.

## Readiness by journey

- Discovery: Beta Hub and closed beta signup explain Eidos as a Presence Platform.
- Trust: Trust Center states the real-voice policy, local-first processing, encryption, consent, export, and deletion in plain language.
- Onboarding: meeting type personalizes the initial presence mode.
- Creation: photo upload and avatar identity generation require consent.
- Practice: post-avatar walkthrough teaches voice-only mode, quality review, privacy, and OBS routing.
- Meeting: dedicated 720p/1080p/square output works with OBS Virtual Camera.
- Recovery: system checks and diagnostic export cover media, GPU, renderer, tracking, output, storage, and encryption.
- Validation: local feedback, PMF, referral, analytics, and Founder Dashboard workflows are available.

## Decision

GO for a controlled closed beta. NO-GO for public self-serve distribution until signing, native virtual camera delivery, external security/privacy review, and real-user validation are complete.
