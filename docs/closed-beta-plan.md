# Eidos Closed Beta Plan

## Purpose

Recruit 10-20 people who attend at least five video meetings per week and have a concrete reason to reduce camera use. Eidos is a Presence Platform: the participant always speaks with their authentic microphone voice; Eidos never clones a voice or generates speech.

## Cohorts

- 5 professional knowledge workers or recruiters
- 3-5 creators or presenters
- 3-5 camera-comfort, accessibility, or low-energy users
- 2-3 enterprise evaluators

Each participant receives three expiring referral codes. Cohort, creator, expiration, and usage are stored locally and can be exported by an administrator.

## Runbook

1. Screen and invite participants with a cohort code.
2. Confirm analytics and diagnostic choices before collecting anything.
3. Complete presence-focused onboarding, avatar creation, and 60-second training.
4. Require five real presence sessions over two weeks, including two voice-only sessions.
5. Collect the naturalness/trust check-in after meaningful sessions.
6. Trigger the PMF survey after session five.
7. Review Founder Dashboard weekly and interview users behind anomalous metrics.

## Exit criteria

Proceed beyond closed beta when at least 40% would be very disappointed, 60% use voice-only mode more than once, median presence quality exceeds 75, onboarding completion exceeds 70%, and critical privacy/crash incidents are zero.
